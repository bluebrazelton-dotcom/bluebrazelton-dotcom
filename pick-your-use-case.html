Part 1: Choose it

The best first use case is work you understand better than anyone and never have time for.

  1. Think forms, deadlines, exceptions, who actually owns what.
  4. Your supervisor, usually. A colleague who shares the task also counts.

Part 2: Scope it

An experiment is safe when the data is allowed, the check step is named, and the supervisor is in the loop.

  1. Check the class under NAU's Data Classification and Handling Policy. When in doubt, start with public data and ask ITS which tools are approved.
  3. New to this: ask for a one-month experiment. Already using agents: propose the measure that says it worked.

The eight moves

Work loop (you and the agent): instruct, run, verify, iterate.
Leadership loop (you and your supervisor): supervise, document, disclose, hand off.
